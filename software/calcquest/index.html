CalcQuest

A small browser RPG about calculation and adventure.

Play CalcQuest → Play in your browser. No download required.

Enemies are numbers. Use calculator keys to make a value exactly equal to their HP and defeat them.

Each victory gives you a new key — and a new way to calculate.

Enemies and bosses change every game. After clearing the game, compare your score with the theoretical best.
