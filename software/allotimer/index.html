allotimer

Not a lot o' time? Allot it.

Randomize your time. See where it actually goes.

Open allotimer → Runs in your browser. No download required.

allotimer randomly allocates your available time across activities based on their priorities, with a little randomness mixed in.

Start the timer, and your plan changes with you: activities that run over their allocation gradually take time from the others, turning the original plan into a picture of where your time actually went.

How it works

01

Add activities

List what you want to do and give each activity a priority.

02

Allot your time

Let weighted randomness turn your available time into a plan.

03

See what happened

Run the timer and watch the plan become your actual time distribution.

Why I made it

I wanted a way to decide how to spend a limited block of free time without planning every minute myself. So I built a weighted-random time allocator that gradually transforms its original budget into your actual time distribution.
