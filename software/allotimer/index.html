allotimer

Not a lot o' time? Allot it.

Randomize your time. See where it actually goes.

Open allotimer → Runs in your browser. No download required.

allotimer helps you divide a limited block of time across activities. Give each activity a WEIGHT, reserve a FIXED amount of time, or combine both in the same plan.

Generate an allocation, lock the times you want to keep, and reroll the rest. Once the timer starts, actual time gradually reshapes the original allocation, turning the plan into a record of where your time really went.

How it works

01

Add activities

List the things you want to do.

02

Plan the time

Use WEIGHT for flexible priorities or FIXED for time you want to reserve.

03

Lock and reroll

Generate a plan, LOCK the allocations you like, and REROLL only the rest.

04

Track and compare

Run the timer as actual time reshapes the allocation. When you finish, compare PLANNED and ACTUAL totals, plus each activity's allocated and actual time.

Why I made it

I wanted a way to decide how to spend a limited block of free time without planning every minute myself. So I built a time allocator that combines fixed commitments with weighted randomness, then gradually transforms its original plan into your actual time distribution.
